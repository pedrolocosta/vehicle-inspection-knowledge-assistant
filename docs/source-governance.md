# Source and approval governance

## Candidate source classes

The working project discusses Inmetro Portarias 147/2022 and 149/2022, relevant CONTRAN/SENATRAN acts, and controlled PTs and ITs. These names indicate where research starts. Before relying on a document, confirm its exact text, amendments, current status, applicability, and effective dates through the issuing authority and the organization's controlled document system.

## Review sequence

1. **Source steward:** records the document's identity, rights, version, official location, and retrieval date.
2. **Extractor:** proposes an atomic requirement and cites its exact location.
3. **RT reviewer:** checks the interpretation, scope, procedure, and outcome against current sources; records approval or rejection.
4. **Publisher:** releases only approved records to the answer index and retains the prior version for auditability.
5. **Change monitor:** flags amendments or document changes and suspends affected answers until re-review where needed.

An approval must identify the reviewed record version. Editing an approved record returns it to review. A general project approval does not approve every individual requirement.

## Conflict handling

If a PT or IT differs from a regulation, capture both and escalate to the RT; do not silently select a convenient rule. If a source cannot be verified or an effective date is uncertain, withhold the operational conclusion.
